Rules of the table

  1. Baseline: Every registered club player starts at 1000 ELO.
  2. Match Length: Standard matches are played to 11 points (must win by at least 2 points).
  3. Match Reporting: Either participant may report the match result immediately following play.
  4. Admin Results: A club admin can add roster players without accounts and record official matches immediately.
  5. Two-Player Confirmation: ELO ratings do not change until both the winner and the loser log in and confirm the result.
  6. Dispute / Rejection: If a match was reported mistakenly or with incorrect score, either player can reject/cancel the pending report.
  7. Rating Conservation: Rating points gained by the winner equal the exact points deducted from the loser.

Elo Rating Formula

Ratings are calculated using the standard Elo rating formula with a K-Factor of 32:

1. Expected Win Probability:
EWinner = 1 / (1 + 10 (Loser Elo − Winner Elo) / 400)

2. Rating Change:
Δ Elo = Round( 32 × (1 − EWinner) )
Interactive Test: If a 1000 Elo player defeats a 1100 Elo player, expected score is ~36%, resulting in a +20 Elo gain!
